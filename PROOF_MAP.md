# Proof chain and verification boundary

This project writes every dimension $n\ge3$ as `n = m + 3`, with `m : ℕ`, and uses the ball of radius $R>0$ at unit coupling. The arrows below describe conditional theorems actually composed in Lean. The profile existence and regularity, the standard sharp sphere gap, the published entire-space minimum against compactly supported perturbations, and the standard zero-trace extension bridge at the starting points of those arrows are supplied as external mathematical inputs.

The uniqueness needed by the paper's argument is no longer left as an unexplained interface. |

The finite-ball equation is used only in the interior; a left-continuous trace handles the outer boundary. |
